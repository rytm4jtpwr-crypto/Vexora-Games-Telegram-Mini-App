---
name: VEX and Gifts safety
description: Product boundary for game currency and collectible Telegram Gifts.
---

VEX may be used for arcade-style stakes only while it remains free, non-purchasable, non-withdrawable, non-transferable, and without real-world value. Telegram Gifts can appear as visual loadouts or companions, but must never be deducted, transferred, rewarded, or lost through gameplay.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved this boundary for Rocket so the game can provide a stake-and-cash-out loop without risking transferable digital assets.

**How to apply:** Keep future game modes, balances, rewards, UI copy, and backend APIs within this boundary unless the product direction is explicitly reconsidered with appropriate legal and platform review.
